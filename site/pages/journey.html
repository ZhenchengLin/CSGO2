How the thinking evolved

The journey

The useful part of a research project is often the moment an assumption stops surviving contact with the evidence.

ENTRY 001

The schema was premature.

A generic GameState was about to encode untested assumptions. The project stepped back to define the prediction question and inspect real source contracts first.

ENTRY 002

The first target needed an auditable answer.

A/B/No Plant replaced a vague first goal such as recognizing an execute. The objective event made labels, leakage checks, baselines, and calibration assessable.

ENTRY 003

A plausible parser field was not enough.

The first site-label audit found disagreement with plant events. Source outputs became claims to check, not automatic ground truth.

ENTRY 004

A bomb event could be true and still be stale.

A drop event describes where the bomb was dropped. After pickup, current inventory is more informative about where it is now. The reconstruction policy changed.

ENTRY 005

Zero spread changed the interpretation, not the arithmetic.

A lone survivor also has a compact geometric footprint. Combat state was needed to distinguish survival from coordinated formation.

ENTRY 006

Useful-sounding information could lose its place.

Current equipment value was tested in both model families. It worsened probability quality, so economy was excluded from the selected baseline.

ENTRY 007

The model and the representation had to be questioned together.

Defense did not improve logistic probability metrics. The nonlinear comparison recovered predictive value, motivating the interaction-dependent interpretation.

ENTRY 008

The errors exposed a different next question.

Most errors crossed Plant/No Plant. Task-specific importance linked combat to feasibility and geometry to site choice, motivating a hierarchical hypothesis.

ENTRY 009

Completion meant freezing a boundary.

The offline investigation became a recorded V0 baseline. Live inference, richer semantics, and improved V1 models remain future claims to earn with new evidence.
