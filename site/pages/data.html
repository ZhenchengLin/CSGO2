Source investigations that mattered

Correctness before scale.

Plant-event labels

The initial demo audit found disagreement between a round-level site field and actual plant events. V0 derives A/B labels from the events.

Inventory before drop history

A previous drop event can be stale after pickup. Current C4 inventory takes precedence when reconstructing the bomb position.

Training and serving must agree

The contract to validate before online inference.

Future adapters must reproduce the same feature meanings and respect the same time cutoff. Position-derived motion avoids relying on parser-specific velocity fields, but actual update cadence, stale state, identity matching, missingness, and latency still need testing.

XGB-A5 uses CT positions, health, armor, and survival from full observer information. It is not validated under an ordinary player’s partial view.
