What the evidence taught us

The findings that shaped the model.

01

Position tells us where. Combat tells us whether.

Offensive geometry dominates conditional A/B site choice. Survival and combat state dominate whether a plant happens at all.

Two subproblemsOne three-class V0 model
02

Defense needed a model that could use interactions.

Defense worsened logistic probability metrics, then improved XGBoost at every tested horizon. Current equipment value did not earn its place.

0.857756 → 0.838182XGB log loss, adding defense
03

The hard boundary is plant versus no plant.

575 of 683 classification errors cross that boundary. This motivates testing a hierarchical V1, whose benefit remains unproven.
